01 Platinum roadmap

One playthrough is enough. There is no mission replay and no New Game+, so save smart.

⚠ The one missable trophy: Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at all 18 spots. Do Lucia's balcony meditation in Chapter 1 (after the mission Down to Earth, find Lucia at Jason's house in the morning and follow her to the balcony). It is gone once Chapter 1 ends. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter, plus one from just before the final mission.

Step 1 · 60–80 hrs

Play the story

Complete all five chapters with Jason and Lucia. Story trophies pop automatically — no gold medals needed. Scan vehicles and study animals with the WAiNK app and binoculars as you go: you will need 200 base-model vehicles and 100 animals later.

Step 2 · ~100 hrs

Reach 100% completion

After the credits, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. Finish the robberies, collectibles, races, 42 Shooting Range golds, 42 Drone Time Trials, 300 stunts and 70 of 80 Challenges. This earns A Long Road (Gold), the hardest trophy.

Step 3 · 10–20 hrs

Clean up the rest

Finish the final missions for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone, complete 8 Strangers & Freaks strands, and mop up any remaining combat, driving and misc trophies. Then the Platinum — Vice Got a Price! — is yours.

    04 Quick answers

    How many trophies does GTA VI have?

    55 on PS5: 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver and 46 Bronze. The same 55 exist as Xbox achievements worth 1,000 Gamerscore. 28 of them are hidden until you unlock them.

    Can I get the Platinum in one playthrough?

    Yes. Every trophy is single-player and there are no online trophies. The only risk is Positive Mental Attitude, which is potentially missable in Chapter 1 — keep manual saves at the start of each chapter.

    How long does the Platinum take?

    About 200 hours, at difficulty 4/10. It is long, not hard: the time goes to 100% completion (A Long Road), 250+ stunts, collectibles and the 42 Shooting Range gold medals. There are no difficulty trophies and no mission medals.

    Do cheats disable trophies?

    Yes — cheats switch off trophies and saving while active, so never use them on your trophy save. Cheats only become available after the story anyway.

    Does this guide work offline?

    Yes. It is a static page with no network requests at all — no CDN, no fonts, no images, no analytics. Your checkmarks are stored in this browser's local storage, so they survive reloads.
